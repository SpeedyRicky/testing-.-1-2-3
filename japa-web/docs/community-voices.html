Japa: The Great Escape · community research · October 2026

Japa voices: what people say about every route to Canada

Recurring stories from Nigerians who tried each pathway between 2022 and 2026, the real rule behind each one, and the scene it became in the game. Game version 0.11 ships everything marked built.

How this was gathered: five research passes covered Reddit, Nairaland, X, YouTube and TikTok stories as reported by Nigerian outlets (Legit.ng, BusinessDay, Punch, Guardian, TheCable, Vanguard) and Canadian outlets (CBC, Global News, Toronto Star, CTV), plus IRCC, IRB and StatCan data. Most forum pages could not be opened directly, so community voice comes through news coverage and search summaries. Everything is paraphrased; no individual is named. Items marked verify came from a single or commercial source.

Entertainment research, not legal or immigration advice.

Year One in Canada (new route)

The most consistent message across every source: landing is not the end. v0.11 adds Year One in Canada, playable on its own and unlocked by permanent residence or refugee protection. It tracks Savings, Career, Community and "Japa blues".

What this adds to the game

Reality check on every endingAll 46 endings now end with one plain-language rule from the real process, shown in the game-over card.
Endings cabinetA header chip counts endings found (0/46) and opens a cabinet grouped by route; hidden endings stay locked. A badge pops when you find a new one.
Mistakes you can recover fromA study refusal is no longer a dead end: GCMS notes let you fix the plan. A PFL can be answered.
Scams as choices, not lecturesGuaranteed visas, LMIA sellers, Facebook landlords and bought stories are offered like real temptations, then show the real cost.
The life afterYear One turns "I landed" into a new run, which is where most real stories actually happen.
