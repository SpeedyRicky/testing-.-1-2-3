## What we removed from the draft copy, and why
- "Embassy Interview": Canada rarely interviews visitor or study applicants. We say "face the officer" instead.
- "Immediate PR" via the Startup Visa: IRCC has paused new Start-up Visa applications.
- "Under 35MB": the whole game is about 1 MB, so we say so.
- "5-year ban" as a selling line: kept only as a consequence of fraud, which is accurate (misrepresentation).
- "IELTS punctuation challenges": IELTS doesn't test that.
