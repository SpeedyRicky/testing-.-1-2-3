## Full description
Think you can survive the ultimate relocation grind?

The Great Japa Escape is a story-and-strategy simulation about the real hurdles of moving from Nigeria to Canada:
money, paperwork, language tests, family, and the cold.

THE GRIND IS REAL
- Beat the clock in rapid-fire IELTS punctuation challenges.
- Spot the fake agent before he spots your savings.
- Pitch investors, face a skeptical employer, and talk your way past a landlord who wants a credit score you do not have.

LANDING IS ONLY THE BEGINNING
- Run two households at once if your family stays behind: a Canadian budget and a Nigerian one, in two currencies.
- Survive shift work, rent day and your first sub-zero winter.
- Keep your stress and your family together while you do it.

PLAY ANYWHERE
- Fully playable offline. No account needed.
- English and Nigerian Pidgin.

This game is a simulation for entertainment. It is not legal or immigration advice. Rules, fees and cutoffs
change often; check IRCC (canada.ca) before you make real decisions.
